Lunaz has a free tier and monthly plans. Lunaz does not take a share of your payments, because it never holds the funds.

All plans include every feature: shielded invoices, cross-chain payments, signed webhooks, the accountant export and withdraw to bank. The plans differ only by volume.

| Free | 0 USD | 100 | 2 | Trials and small shops |

| Platform | 199 USD | Unlimited | 50 | Marketplaces with many sellers |
| Enterprise | Contact us | Custom | Custom | Service levels and compliance |

## What counts as an invoice

Each call to `POST /v1/invoices` that succeeds counts as one invoice in the current month. The month starts at 00:00 UTC on the first day. Invoices that pay for a Lunaz plan do not count.

An API key counts while it is active. A revoked key does not count.

## When you reach a limit

The API returns 402 with the `limit`, the `used` count and your `plan`. Existing invoices keep working. See [Errors](../api/errors/).

## Pay for a plan

You pay in ZEC through a Lunaz invoice. The dashboard creates a normal invoice for the Lunaz treasury wallet. When the invoice is paid, the plan is active for the paid period.

- You can buy 1, 3 or 12 months.
- The amount is the USD price converted at the ZEC price when you start the checkout.
- The checkout invoice is valid for 30 minutes.
- A month is 30 days.

### Upgrades and renewals

An upgrade starts at once. There is no proration. A renewal of the same plan adds the new period after the current period ends.

Renewal is manual. There is no automatic charge. To renew, pay a new invoice. When a plan expires, the account goes back to Free, and Lunaz sends a `plan.updated` webhook.
