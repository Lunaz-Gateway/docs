Lunaz is a managed service. You pay only when you get paid. There are no monthly plans, no setup fee, no invoice limits and no API key limits. Every feature is available to every merchant: shielded invoices, cross-chain payments, signed webhooks, the accountant export and withdraw to bank.

| Payment | Lunaz fee | Who pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shielded ZEC | 0.5% (minimum 1,000 zatoshis, 0.00001 ZEC) | The buyer, on top of the price |
| Cross-chain (NEAR Intents) | 0.25% app fee on the swap | The buyer, inside the deposit amount |

Lunaz takes no fee from the amount that you receive.

## Shielded payments

The `zip321_uri` of an invoice is a payment request with two payments in one shielded transaction:

1. The amount of the invoice, to the invoice address of the merchant.
2. The Lunaz fee, to a Lunaz address.

The buyer pays the fee on top of the price. The fee goes from the buyer to Lunaz in the same transaction. Lunaz never holds or touches your funds. The fee output is shielded, so it is private too.

<Aside type="caution">
Always show the `zip321_uri` to the buyer, as a QR code or a link. Do not build your own request from the address and the amount. If you do, the fee is lost.
</Aside>

If a buyer's wallet pays only the merchant part, the invoice is still marked `paid`. The buyer is never blocked. Lunaz bills the missing fee to the merchant later.

## Cross-chain payments

A buyer who pays with another asset pays a 0.25% app fee on the swap. The fee is part of the deposit amount. The merchant still receives the full invoice amount. See [Accept other crypto](../guides/accept-other-crypto/).
