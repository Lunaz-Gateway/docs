You can convert ZEC to a local currency, for example IDR, through a licensed off-ramp partner. You are the customer of the partner. The partner does the identity check and pays your bank account.

Lunaz creates the order, shows the deposit details and tracks the status. Lunaz never holds your funds. It keeps only the bank name and the last 4 digits of the account number.

:::caution
THE DEPOSIT ADDRESS IS A TRANSPARENT ADDRESS. THE ZEC LEAVES THE SHIELDED POOL, AND THE PARTNER CAN LINK THE WITHDRAWAL TO YOUR ACCOUNT.
:::

## Withdraw

<Steps>

1. Open the Withdraw page in the dashboard.

2. Enter the amount of ZEC.

3. Select the currency.

   The dashboard shows a quote with the rate, the fees and the amount that you receive.

4. Create the withdrawal.

5. Complete the identity check and select your bank in the partner step.

6. Send the exact amount of ZEC from your wallet to the deposit address.

   :::caution
   DO NOT SEND FUNDS IF THE WITHDRAWAL SHOWS SANDBOX. THE MOCK DEPOSIT ADDRESS IS NOT A VALID ZCASH ADDRESS.
   :::

7. Wait for the status `completed`.

   The partner sends the money to your bank account.

</Steps>

## Quote and fees

A quote is valid for 10 minutes. The minimum is 100000 zat. In the mock, the partner fee is 1.5% and the network fee is 0.0001 ZEC. The rates in the mock are indicative and are not real rates.

The received total on the Withdraw page counts the payments that Lunaz detected. It is not the balance of your wallet.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `awaiting_kyc` | You must complete the identity check and select a bank. |
| `awaiting_deposit` | The partner waits for your ZEC. |
| `deposit_received` | The partner received your ZEC. |
| `converting` | The partner converts ZEC to fiat. |
| `payout_processing` | The partner sends the money to your bank. |
| `completed` | The money is paid. |
| `expired` | The quote or the deposit window passed. |
| `failed` | The withdrawal failed. |
| `cancelled` | You cancelled the withdrawal. |

You can cancel a withdrawal before `deposit_received`. Each status change sends a `withdrawal.updated` webhook. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks/).

<Aside type="note">
The status `quote` is never stored. It exists only in the quote step.
</Aside>
